Championship Banner — Unity Cloth prototype

Source: ChampionshipBanner_Source.blend (standalone Blender scene).
Unity asset folder: Assets/Project/Content/Props/ChampionshipBanner
Test scene: Assets/Project/Dev/Sandbox/BannerCloth/SC_BannerCloth.unity
Rebuild: BuyBidBlow > Banner Cloth > Build Test Scene

6 m wide, 2 m high; 60 x 20 grid cells; 1,281 vertices; 2,400 triangles.
Single surface with continuous 0–1 UV; no solidify, baked cloth or duplicate back face.
Unity material renders both sides. Reverse side shows mirrored print like a thin printed fabric.
Origin is top center. Four vertices in the two top corner patches are pinned.
Free vertex travel is limited from 0.7 m at the top to 1.5 m at the bottom
to retain title readability. Adjust Cloth constraints to allow a looser hang.
Cloth is on a SkinnedMeshRenderer; bones are not needed for this fixed hanging banner.
Keep the transform scale at 1 for the initial physical test.

Open the test scene and press Play. Capsule automatically passes through the fabric.
Turn off Automatic capsule contact to move the capsule with arrow keys.
Wind slider controls acceleration; Unity WindZone is not automatically connected.

The prefab contains cloth and material; scene-specific character colliders must be
assigned to Cloth > Capsule Colliders or Sphere Colliders in the target scene.
Unity Cloth receives contact from these explicit colliders; it does not push the
character back, and arbitrary mesh colliders are not supported by this setup.
This prototype does not add network synchronization or wire into the real player prefab.
Blender is used only for mesh/UV authoring; physics is evaluated in Unity Play Mode.
