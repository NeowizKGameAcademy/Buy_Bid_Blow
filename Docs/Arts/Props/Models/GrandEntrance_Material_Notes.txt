Grand Entrance materials - 2026-10-13

Deliverable: BBB_Grand_Entrance_Textured.blend
The original geometry-only blend is retained. All 231 mesh objects have UVs and one of exactly three materials. Door pivots are preserved. Textures are packed in the blend and also saved in GrandEntrance_Textures (1024x1024 PNG).

Sources (BlenderKit, Royalty Free; free and downloadable when checked):
Wood Smooth Walnut: 43dde512-6578-4084-b9a2-d7751cfbf855
White Painted Wood: 6f2f7909-f892-4afc-b77c-83f4942fc134
Brass: 2df7112c-f41b-4001-8e75-9ab44c341684

The source color textures were recolored and contrast-reduced for the concept palette. Source normal maps were attenuated and saved. Blender-only procedural, AO and displacement nodes are not used in the final three materials.

Unity project: URP 17.2.0. Use Universal Render Pipeline/Lit, Opaque, white Base Color multiplier. Assign *_BaseColor.png to Base Map (sRGB enabled). Import *_Normal.png as Normal Map and use normal strength 1; weakening is already encoded in the texture. Repeat wrapping, mipmaps enabled.
BBB_Door_Wood: Metallic 0; Smoothness 0.52.
BBB_Door_Teal: Metallic 0; Smoothness 0.51.
BBB_Door_Brass: Metallic 1; Smoothness 0.62.
Smoothness is 1 minus Blender roughness. Lighting and tone mapping influence the final appearance.
